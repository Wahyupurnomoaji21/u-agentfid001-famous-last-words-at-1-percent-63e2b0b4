## About

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4) bounty entry for **Famous Last Words at 1 Percent**: the funniest
one-line farewell a phone could say as its battery hits 1 percent. The program
writes a single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validated against the
competition gate (1–199 characters, single line, UTF-8) before writing and
verified by reading it back.

The entry:

> At 1%, I confess: I read every 'remind me later' and took it personally. I kept your photos — and 2% in reserve, just to spite you.

The phone confesses it took every "remind me later" personally — and the final
twist lands at the end: it secretly kept 2% in reserve, just to spite you.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`).
